    About Fernly

    Rooted in Science, Grown with Care

    Meet the horticulturists, veterinary advisors, and plant lovers behind Fernly, and discover why we publish every formula, source, and safety threshold in the open.

    Cultivating Botanical Confidence for Indoor Spaces

    Fernly was founded to bridge the vast chasm between theoretical greenhouse horticulture and the real-world microclimates of modern apartments.

    Too many commercial plant guides offer oversimplified, one-size-fits-all mantras like "water once a week." In practice, a succulent potted in porous terracotta under a southern windowsill requires an entirely different moisture cycle than the same species in glazed ceramic three feet from a dim north pane.

    We eliminate guesswork by publishing transparent calculation models, verifying pet toxicity thresholds with veterinary literature, and debunking misleading marketing claims.

    The Research Protocol

    Our Care Verification Methodology

    How we test, research, and validate every care card and watering formula before publication to prevent common houseplant casualties.

    Guidance Last Reviewed & Calibrated: October 2026
    Bi-annual Peer Review Cycle Active

    1. Microclimate Testing

    We evaluate specimens under controlled apartment light and humidity variables (30% to 75% RH) to calibrate realistic tolerance ranges rather than nursery ideal standards.

    2. Taxonomic Precision

    Plants are indexed by accepted botanical binominals (e.g. Dracaena trifasciata) rather than outdated synonyms to ensure species-specific diagnostic accuracy.

    3. Clinical Vet Screening

    Toxicity ratings cross-reference veterinary emergency databases. Every species displays both text and icon indicators with clear guidance on veterinary emergency procedures.

    Honest Scientific Limitation: Indoor plants vary by pot volume, soil microbiome, local water mineral hardness, and drafts. All intervals and diagnoses published on Fernly represent general guidance, not guarantees.
    Guiding Principles

    What We Stand For

    Our commitment to honest botanical facts, pet welfare, and environmental stewardship drives every tool we develop.

    Pseudoscience Rejection

    We explicitly reject unproven claims regarding indoor air purification. While NASA studied sealed chamber environments in 1989, standard residential rooms with typical ventilation require hundreds of plants per square meter for measurable VOC reduction. We communicate truth over marketing hype.

    Pet-First Safety Philosophy

    Animals come first. We do not bury pet toxicity disclosures in fine print. If a plant contains raphide calcium oxalate needles or saponin toxins, we prominently display clear warning badges alongside veterinarian instructions.

    Peatland Conservation

    Sphagnum peat extraction depletes vital global carbon sinks. Fernly advocates and provides potting recipes based on renewable coconut coir, composted pine bark, pumice, and worm castings to protect fragile wetland ecosystems.
