    Annual Rhythm

    Seasonal Houseplant Care Transitions

    Houseplants react directly to changing day length and central heating seasons. Adjust your watering and feeding protocols as the calendar turns.

    March – May (Northern Hemisphere)
    As daylight lengthens, root respiration and leaf bud production surge.
    Watering Transition

    Soil dries noticeably faster as plants wake. Increase check frequency from bi-weekly to weekly.

    Fertilizer Resumption

    Begin feeding with half-strength balanced liquid fertilizer every 3–4 weeks to fuel fresh shoots.

    Repotting Window

    Spring is the ideal repotting season. Move root-bound plants to pots 1–2 inches wider before summer.

    Pest Inspection

    Scout new tender leaf tips for emerging aphids, fungus gnats, or spider mites waking up with warmer days.
